The course

An out-and-back along the Santa Fe River Trail

Start at Romero’s Park in Agua Fría, loop the village, then head out the river corridor to the turnaround at mile 9 and run back the same road to the finish at Reunity Resources Farm.

Start · Romero’s Park, Agua Fría Finish · Reunity Resources Farm
  • 13.1 miOut-and-back course
  • +651 ftTotal climb
  • 5 stationsWater & electrolytes
  • 4.5 hrCourse limit

It’s paved the whole way, but the altitude is real. Go out easy, drink early and often, and save something for the pull up to the mile-9 turnaround — the run home is downhill from there. Profile is illustrative; the official course map comes with your race guide.

Plan your weekend

Everything you need before race morning

A few honest pointers from our team so you arrive ready and leave wanting to come back.

Beat the altitude

Coming from sea level? Get here two to three days early, skip the hard efforts, and keep a water bottle glued to your hand.

Getting here

Fly into Albuquerque (ABQ) and drive an hour north, or land right at Santa Fe Regional (SAF). Easy either way.

Packet pickup

Grab your bib and shirt at the Saturday expo, 10am–5:30pm. No race-morning pickup, so plan to swing by.

Where to stay

Parking on race morning was at Romero’s Park for the Half and Relay, at Reunity for the 5K, with a third lot at San Isidro Church. Santa Fe Trails ran one shuttle bus on roughly 15-minute loops.

Our story

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How should I prepare for the 7,000 ft altitude?

If you're coming from lower elevation, the altitude is no joke. Arrive two to three days early if you can, drink way more water than feels normal in the days before, and plan to run the first few miles easier than your goal pace. You'll thank yourself at the finish.

When does registration close?

For 2026 it closed early: online entry shut on Thursday, 17 September at 11:59 PM MDT and the race sold out on the 19th, with no walk-up or race-day entry for any distance. Entry for 18–19 September 2027 is not open yet.

Where do the races start and finish?

The Half and Relay start at Romero’s Park in Agua Fría and finish at Reunity Resources Farm, 1829 San Ysidro Crossing — an out-and-back with 651 ft of climb to a 6,883 ft turnaround at mile 9. The Santa Fe 5K and Kids 1K Dash ran shorter loops based at Reunity Resources Farm.

Can my kids run?

Absolutely — the Kids 1K Dash is free for ages 3–12, it is not chip-timed, and every finisher gets a medal. Strollers are welcome in the Santa Fe 5K; just line up toward the back so everyone has room.

What's included with my entry?

A finisher medal made by Maasai artisans, a race shirt carrying Gasali Adeyemo’s adire pattern, five aid stations, course support, and the green chile brunch from Santa Fe YouthWorks at Reunity Resources Farm.
