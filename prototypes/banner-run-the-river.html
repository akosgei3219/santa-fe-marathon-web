September 20, 2026 · Santa Fe International Half Marathon

Thin Air. Big Sky. Run Santa Fe. Run the River.

13.1 miles on the roads of Agua Fría and out the Santa Fe River Trail — the path the restoration built — to a 6,883 ft turnaround and back. Not a closed-off spectacle: a working village of homes, acequias and small farms that opens its streets for one morning.
