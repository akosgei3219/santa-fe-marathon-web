The River Loop Course

“The Santa Fe River trail will dance your shadows like the water dancers by a river… and will tell times on the loop course.”

A high-desert road course through the working village of Agua Fría, out the Santa Fe River corridor to the 6,883 ft turnaround at mile 9 — and back. All of it at 7,000 ft, with +651 ft of climb.

Race-day shuttle by Santa Fe Trails: continuous loops between the three lots and the start, morning and late-morning windows. Times and stops on the Transportation & Parking page.
