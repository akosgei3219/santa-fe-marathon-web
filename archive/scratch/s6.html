Race Concierge

Try: shuttle, packet pickup, altitude, awards, burritos

Open pageAsk anything about race weekendNo match for that yet - the race team can answer directly.
Registration is on RunSignup. Race day is Sunday, September 20, 2026.Half and Relay start 7:30 AM at Romero's Park; every race finishes at Reunity Resources. Best viewing: the Santa Fe River trail crossings and the finish-line festival.Post-race celebration and awards at Reunity Resources Farm, 1829 San Ysidro Crossing, 9:00 AM-1:00 PM: live New Mexico music and a green chile brunch from Santa Fe YouthWorks, then happy hour at Nuckolls Brewing Company.The Host City Guide covers altitude, lodging, and race week in Santa Fe.Packet pickup and expo: Warehouse 21 in the Railyard, Friday 12-6 PM and Saturday 10 AM-5 PM. Race-morning pickup for the Half Marathon and 3-Amigos Relay is 6:00-7:00 AM at Romero's Park - the start line, not the Railyard.The free shuttle, provided by Santa Fe Trails, loops the 3 lots about every 15 minutes, 6:00-7:45 AM to the start (returns 10:00-11:30 AM). Roads into the start close at 7:00 AM. Parking split: Half/Relay at Romero's Park, 5K at Reunity Resources. It is a single bus - if it is full, it returns on the next loop, so leave a cushion. ADA boarding assistance provided.Half and Relay start 7:30 AM at Romero's Park; the Santa Fe 5K starts 8:00 AM at Reunity; the free Kids 1K Dash is Sunday 9:00 AM at Reunity.High-desert course with a 6,883 ft turnaround at mile 9 and +651 ft of climbing. Maps at /course-map/.Santa Fe sits at 7,000 feet. Arrive 2-3 days early if you can, hydrate all week, and expect a slower pace than at sea level.The 3-Amigos Relay starts 7:30 AM at Romero's Park alongside the half. Teams hand off a sash at Corridor Entry (mile 4.9) and the mile-9 turnaround, and Runner 3 finishes at Reunity Resources.The free Kids 1K Dash starts Sunday at 9:00 AM at Reunity Resources.Live results post as runners finish, and official race photos are posted on the Results & Photos page after the race.Registrations are non-refundable. The free deferral and bib-transfer window closed on September 10, 2026.Gear bag drop is 6:00-7:00 AM at Romero's Park - details on the Gear Bag Drop page.The half marathon has a 4 hour 30 minute course limit.Five aid stations cover the 13.1-mile course, with water at miles 2, 4, 6, 8, 9, 10 and 12.Finisher medals and age-group medallions are both handmade by Maasai artisans - every finisher in the Half, Relay and 5K receives one. Awards: 5K at 9:45 AM, Half and Relay at 10:45 AM.Expect crisp high-desert mornings, often around 45 F, warming toward the low 70s by midday - dress in layers you can leave in your gear bag.Charity partners and fundraising details are on the Charity Information page.Volunteer sign-up for race weekend roles is open.The start line sits in the historic village of Agua Fria and the course follows the Santa Fe River trail corridor. Post-race food is a green chile brunch prepared by Santa Fe YouthWorks, and the medallions are handmade by Maasai artisans.The race runs the Santa Fe River corridor - the river the city is named for - through high desert at 7,000 feet, topping out at 6,883 feet at the mile-9 turnaround with 651 feet of climbing.Reach the race team through the contact page.
