## 1. What Mwangaza is

- **Mwangaza Athletics Club, in Santa Fe, New Mexico.** "Mwangaza" means light in
  Swahili (a translation, fine to use). Why the club has that name is Kosgei's to say;
  don't invent a reason.
- **A youth running development project at its heart** (15 Sep: "Mwangaza athletics
  club is to developing youth running project").
- **Adults too** (15 Sep: "And Adults too"). The club is a running club for all ages,
  in Santa Fe or online (15 Sep: "joint our running club in santa fe or online. all
  ages").
- **Training trips to Kenya**, run by Mwangaza, for adults only, to train alongside
  former elite athletes (15 Sep: "run with informer Elites Athletics"; later "Mwangaza
  runs the Kenya trips, adults only").
- Older service lines on the live site still stand behind these: sports diplomacy,
  corporate wellness (retreats), event management, high-altitude coaching.
