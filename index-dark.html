The Course Profile

A road race through a working village and along the river path. You'll start at Romero's Park, loop through Agua Fría, head out the Santa Fe River Trail corridor to the turnaround at mile 9, then run back the same road to the finish at Reunity Resources Farm. It is 100% paved, and it climbs 651 feet in total.

START

The Starting Line

Romero's Park, Agua Fría. Gear bag drop and race-morning packet pickup run 6:00–7:00 AM. Roads into the start close at 7:00 AM. Wheelchair division rolls at 7:25 AM; Half Marathon and 3-Amigos Relay at 7:30 AM.

04

Mile 4 · Medic

A medic is stationed here. Water is on course at miles 2, 4, 6, 8, 9, 10 and 12 across five aid stations.

09

Mile 9 · The Turnaround

6,883 feet, the high point of the day, out on the Santa Fe River Trail corridor. ALS medical is stationed at the turnaround. From here you retrace the same road home.

FIN

The Finish

Reunity Resources Farm, 1829 San Ysidro Crossing — every race finishes here. Medical tent and ALS on site. Course limit is 4 hours 30 minutes and support ends at 12:00 PM; anyone behind the sweep is offered a ride in.

The Santa Fe 5K

5 kilometers • 8:00 AM • Starts & finishes at Reunity Resources Farm

The short course of the morning. Runners and walkers cross the mat at Reunity Resources Farm, 1829 San Ysidro Crossing, and come back into the same finish-line festival the half marathoners run into. Parking for the 5K is at Reunity.

Terrain note

Paved the whole way and far shorter than the half — the friendliest distance on the schedule for a first race or a family morning. You are still at 7,000 feet, so start easy.

Kids 1K Dash FAQ

Who made this year's shirt and medals?
The 2026 race shirt carries a pattern by Gasali Adeyemo, the Santa Fe–based master of Yoruba adire. The finisher medal and the age-group medallions are both made by Maasai artisans. Post-race food is a green chile brunch cooked by Santa Fe YouthWorks.
How do I register my child for the 1K Dash?
The Dash is free, but registration is closed. Online entry closed Thursday, September 17, and the race is sold out — there are no walk-up or race-day entries for any distance, the Kids 1K included.
Can parents run along with their children?
Absolutely. Parents and guardians are fully encouraged to jog alongside younger runners. Strollers, wagons, and family cheering crews are welcome around the festival loop at Reunity Resources Farm.
Can I pick up my adult half marathon packet at the same time?
Yes. Packet pickup runs at the Santa Fe Running Hub, 1100 Don Diego Ave # B, on Friday 12:00–6:00 PM and Saturday 10:00 AM–5:00 PM. If you are running the Half or the Relay you can also collect race morning, 6:00–7:00 AM at Romero's Park, before the Dash starts at 9:00 AM.

Plan your weekend

Planning for 2027

The advice does not change much year to year. Save this for September 18–19, 2027.

Beat the altitude

Coming from sea level? Get here two to three days early, skip the hard efforts, and keep a water bottle glued to your hand.

Getting here

Fly into Albuquerque (ABQ) and drive an hour north, or land right at Santa Fe Regional (SAF).

Packet pickup

Santa Fe Running Hub, 1100 Don Diego Ave # B — Friday 12–6, Saturday 10–5. Half and Relay runners can also collect at Romero's Park on race morning, 6–7 AM.

Where to stay

Parking on race morning is at Romero's Park for the Half and Relay, at Reunity for the 5K, with a third lot at San Isidro Church. Santa Fe Trails runs one shuttle bus on roughly 15-minute loops, 6:00–7:45 AM and again 10:00–11:30 AM, with ADA boarding assistance. Easiest way to stay close: grab a room from our race-rate room block — the SHM rate is already applied. Want to shop around? Try Concept Hotel Group or the Pecos Trail Inn.
