FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE - September 19, 2026

RACE-EVE UPDATE: SANTA FE INTERNATIONAL HALF MARATHON PACKET PICKUP IS AT
THE SANTA FE RUNNING HUB TODAY - RACE DAY UNCHANGED FOR SUNDAY

Traveling runners: pick up your bib TODAY, 10 AM-5 PM, at 1100 Don Diego Ave,
Suite B. The Health & Wellness Expo is cancelled; do not go to Warehouse 21.
Walk-in registration is open at pickup for every race.

SANTA FE, N.M. - September 19, 2026 - One day before the fifth annual Capitol
Ford Santa Fe International Half Marathon, organizers have an important update
for the hundreds of runners arriving in Santa Fe today - and one reassurance:
everything about race day itself is unchanged.

PACKET PICKUP - TODAY, NEW LOCATION
Packet pickup is at the SANTA FE RUNNING HUB, 1100 Don Diego Ave, Suite B,
Santa Fe, NM 87505, TODAY (Saturday) from 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM MT. The Health &
Wellness Expo is cancelled so the City of Santa Fe can use the Warehouse 21
space for temporary emergency shelter - please do NOT go to Warehouse 21 or
the Railyard; nothing race-related is happening there.

Half Marathon and 3-Amigos Relay runners who cannot make it today can still
collect race morning, 6:00-7:00 AM at Romero's Park. Santa Fe 5K runners must
pick up today at the Running Hub.

REGISTRATION - ONLINE CLOSED, WALK-INS WELCOME
Online registration has closed. Anyone who missed it can register IN PERSON
for any race at packet pickup today (pay on site, cash or card). On race
morning, the Santa Fe 5K also takes walk-ins at its start line at Reunity
Resources Farm until 7:45 AM.

RACE DAY - SUNDAY, SEPTEMBER 20, EXACTLY AS PLANNED (ALL TIMES MT)
- 7:25 AM  Wheelchair division (handcycle and push-rim), Romero's Park
- 7:30 AM  Half Marathon & 3-Amigos Relay, Romero's Park, Agua Fria
- 8:00 AM  Santa Fe 5K, Reunity Resources Farm
- 9:00 AM  Free Kids 1K Dash (ages 3-12), Reunity Resources Farm
All races finish at Reunity Resources Farm, 1829 San Ysidro Crossing. Awards:
5K at 9:45 AM, Half Marathon and Relay at 10:45 AM. Course limit 4 hours 30
minutes; on-course support ends at 12:00 PM.

GETTING THERE ON RACE MORNING
Roads into the start close at 7:00 AM sharp. In your maps app, search
"Romero Park, Santa Fe" - street addresses for the park do not work in
navigation apps. Half and Relay runners park at Romero's Park, 5K runners at
Reunity Resources Farm, with overflow parking at San Isidro Church. A Santa Fe
Trails shuttle loops between the lots 6:00-7:45 AM (last departure 7:30) and
again 10:00-11:30 AM (last pickup 11:15). Gear bag drop for Half and Relay
runners: 6:00-7:00 AM at Romero's Park.

A NOTE FOR ARRIVING RUNNERS
Santa Fe sits at 7,000 feet, and the course climbs to 6,883 feet at the mile-9
turnaround on the Santa Fe River Trail. If you arrived this week, drink more
water than feels necessary, start conservatively, and let the altitude set
your pace.
