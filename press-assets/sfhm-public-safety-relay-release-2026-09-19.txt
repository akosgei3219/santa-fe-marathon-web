FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE - September 19, 2026

LOCAL FIRST RESPONDERS SET TO BATTLE FOR THE PUBLIC SAFETY TROPHY AT THE
5TH ANNUAL SANTA FE INTERNATIONAL HALF MARATHON

Six three-person crews from five fire and police agencies race the 3-Amigos
Relay tomorrow morning - and the traveling trophy is up for grabs.

SANTA FE, N.M. - September 19, 2026 - Tomorrow morning, the roads of Agua
Fria Village and the Santa Fe River Trail will host a fierce but friendly
rivalry, as regional first responders face off in the 3-Amigos Relay at the
5th Annual Capitol Ford Santa Fe International Half Marathon.

Thanks to coordination by Los Alamos Fire Chief Eric Litzenberg, the
public-safety division has locked in a competitive field of six teams - 18
tactical and emergency personnel in three-member squads, drawn from five
agencies - who will tackle the 13.1-mile, 7,000-foot high-altitude course to
decide who takes home the coveted traveling Public Safety Trophy.

This year's public-safety starting grid:

- SANTA FE FIRE DEPARTMENT - the reigning champions, back on the course to
  defend the title and keep the trophy at the station.
- SANTA FE COUNTY FIRE DEPARTMENT - fielding a focused squad aiming to
  outrun their city counterparts.
- LOS ALAMOS FIRE DEPARTMENT - coming down the mountain with their own
  high-altitude training in their legs.
- SANTA FE POLICE DEPARTMENT - a strong contingent determined to bring the
  trophy to the precinct.
- SANTA FE FIRE ACADEMY - fresh recruits eager to test their grit, stamina,
  and teamwork against the veterans.

The relay splits the half marathon course into legs of 4.9, 4.1 and 4.1
miles, with exchange zones along the Santa Fe River corridor - and a sash,
not a baton, passed hand to hand from the start gun to the finish line.

The public-safety teams join hundreds of half marathon and 5K runners from
across the region. Racing starts tomorrow (Sunday, September 20) at Romero's
Park in Agua Fria - wheelchair division 7:25 AM, Half Marathon and 3-Amigos
Relay 7:30 AM - with the Santa Fe 5K at 8:00 AM and the free Kids 1K Dash at
9:00 AM at Reunity Resources Farm. Every race finishes at the community
celebration at Reunity Resources Farm, 1829 San Ysidro Crossing, where relay
awards are presented at 10:45 AM.

Spectators, families, and neighbors are encouraged to gather along the
course and at the farm to cheer on these local emergency crews as they trade
their uniforms for running shoes in the name of community health and
camaraderie. Note for race morning: roads into the start close at 7:00 AM -
in a maps app, search "Romero Park, Santa Fe" rather than a street address.
