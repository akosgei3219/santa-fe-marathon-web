FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE - September 19, 2026

FIFTH ANNUAL SANTA FE INTERNATIONAL HALF MARATHON OFFICIALLY SOLD OUT;
LOCAL FIRST RESPONDERS READY FOR RELAY CHALLENGE

SANTA FE, N.M. - September 19, 2026 - Race organizers announced today that
the 5th Annual Capitol Ford Santa Fe International Half Marathon has reached
maximum capacity and is completely sold out. With more than 400 participants
locked in across all weekend events - including an impressive 34% draw of
out-of-state athletes representing 30 different states - the course is primed
for a historic turnout.

Because the event has reached full capacity, organizers emphasize that NO
race-day or walk-up registration will be available at the start line tomorrow
morning.

Tomorrow's high-altitude lines will also feature a fierce but friendly
community rivalry, as regional first responders face off in the 3-Amigos
Relay division. Thanks to extensive coordination and outreach by Los Alamos
County Fire Chief Erik Litzenberg, the public-safety division has secured a
competitive field of six teams - 18 tactical and emergency personnel in
three-member squads, drawn from five agencies - who will tackle the
challenging 7,000-foot course to determine who takes home the coveted,
traveling Public Safety Trophy.

This year's public-safety starting grid:

- SANTA FE FIRE DEPARTMENT: The reigning champions, returning to active duty
  on the course to defend their title and keep the trophy at the station.
- SANTA FE COUNTY FIRE DEPARTMENT: Fielding a highly focused, elite squad
  aiming to outperform their city counterparts.
- LOS ALAMOS FIRE DEPARTMENT: Traveling down the mountain to bring their
  high-altitude training to the competitive field.
- SANTA FE POLICE DEPARTMENT: Deploying a strong contingent determined to
  secure the trophy for the precinct.
- SANTA FE FIRE ACADEMY: An energetic group of fresh recruits eager to test
  their grit, stamina, and teamwork against veteran first responders.

The 3-Amigos Relay joins hundreds of half marathon, 5K, and kids' runners
arriving from across the region and the country. Racing starts tomorrow
(Sunday, September 20) at Romero's Park in Agua Fria - wheelchair division
7:25 AM, Half Marathon and 3-Amigos Relay 7:30 AM - sending runners along
the scenic Santa Fe River Trail corridor before culminating in a vibrant
community celebration at the Reunity Resources Farm finish festival, 1829
San Ysidro Crossing.

Race founder Coach Kosgei captured the spirit of the grueling high-altitude
environment: "This racecourse will for sure dance with your shadow, and time
will tell."

The Race Committee extends its deepest gratitude to the corporate and local
sponsors whose generosity fuels this event, and to the Santa Fe Police
Department and the Santa Fe County Sheriff's Office for ensuring a secure,
well-coordinated course. Special thanks are also due to the dedicated
volunteers managing aid stations and logistics, the stewards of the Santa Fe
River Trail, the historic Agua Fria community, and all local residents for
graciously hosting this annual tradition.

Neighbors, families, and visitors are warmly invited to line the pathways
tomorrow morning to cheer on the athletes and welcome the runners through
the final miles. Roads into the start close at 7:00 AM - spectators should
search "Romero Park, Santa Fe" in a maps app rather than a street address.
