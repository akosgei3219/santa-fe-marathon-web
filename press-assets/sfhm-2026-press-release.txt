FIFTH ANNUAL SANTA FE INTERNATIONAL HALF MARATHON RETURNS SEPTEMBER 18-20
WITH A NEW COURSE THROUGH AGUA FRIA VILLAGE

Half Marathon, 3-Amigos Relay and 5K now finish at Reunity Resources Farm;
race weekend kicks off downtown at the Santa Fe Railyard with a two-day expo,
a panel on adaptive sports, and the Kids 1K Dash.

SANTA FE, N.M. — September 3, 2026 — The Capitol Ford Santa Fe International
Half Marathon returns for its fifth annual running the weekend of September
18-20, and this year the race looks different. For the first time, the Half
Marathon and the 3-Amigos Relay start at Romero's Park in the historic village
of Agua Fria and finish at Reunity Resources Farm, trading the downtown start
for a route that follows the Santa Fe River corridor through one of the city's
oldest communities.

Race morning is Sunday, September 20. The wheelchair division — handcycle and
push-rim — rolls out at 7:25 a.m. MT, with the Half Marathon and Relay following
at 7:30. The 5K starts at 8:00 a.m. from Reunity Resources Farm and loops
through Agua Fria Village before returning to the same finish line the half
marathoners cross. Every distance runs at elevation: the course tops out at
6,883 feet at the mile-9 turnaround, making it one of the highest half
marathons in the country.

The party at the finish runs from 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. at Reunity Resources
Farm, with food, music, and the awards ceremony at 11:00 a.m.
Post-race food is served in partnership with Santa Fe YouthWorks, and
age-group winners take home medallions handcrafted by Maasai artisans.

A RACE WEEKEND, NOT JUST A RACE

The weekend opens downtown. The Health & Wellness Expo and packet pickup run
Friday, September 18 from 12:00 to 6:00 p.m. and Saturday, September 19 from
10:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. at Warehouse 21 in the Santa Fe Railyard. Saturday at
1:00 p.m., the "Catalyst for Change" panel brings together athletes, advocates
and health leaders on adaptive sports and global wellness. And new this year,
the Kids 1K Dash moves to race morning: Sunday at 9:00 a.m. at Reunity
Resources Farm, in the middle of the finish-line festival, with a medal for
every young finisher.

The weekend's cultural thread starts early — with the youngest runners: Gasali
Adeyemo, the Santa Fe-based master of Yoruba adire textile art, leads a free
hands-on indigo dyeing workshop for Kids 1K Dash runners on Sunday, September
13 at Reunity Resources — a week before race weekend — where kids create their
own hand-dyed race shirts using techniques passed down through generations.

"For our fifth anniversary, we wanted the course to feel deeply connected to
local heritage — winding through Agua Fria Village down to the farm. It's
incredibly meaningful to see this event grow into a platform where community
connection, high-desert performance, and global traditions all run together on
the same path."
— Abraham Kosgei, race founder

KNOW BEFORE YOU GO

There is no shuttle this year. Half Marathon and Relay participants park at
Romero's Park; 5K runners park at Reunity Resources Farm, where their race
starts and finishes. Both lots are limited, and roads into the start close at
7:00 a.m. sharp — organizers ask runners to arrive early and carpool where
they can. Race-morning packet pickup at Romero's Park (6:00-7:00 a.m.) is
available for the Half and Relay only.

Registration for all distances is open at
https://runsignup.com/Race/Register/?raceId=83604. Full course maps, schedules
and logistics are at https://santafehalfmarathon.com.

ABOUT THE SANTA FE INTERNATIONAL HALF MARATHON

The Santa Fe International Half Marathon is a community-rooted race weekend in
Santa Fe, New Mexico, presented by Capitol Ford with support from the City of
Santa Fe Economic Development Department. Run at 7,000 feet in the high
desert, the event pairs a Half Marathon, 3-Amigos Relay, 5K and Kids 1K Dash
with a two-day health and wellness expo, and channels race weekend into
partnerships with local organizations including Santa Fe YouthWorks and
Reunity Resources.
