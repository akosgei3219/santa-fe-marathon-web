1Race overview

  • Race day: Sunday, September 20, 2026 · Santa Fe, New Mexico · 7,000 ft. The course runs between 6,567 and 6,883 ft, with 651 ft of climbing.
  • Start times: wheelchair division 7:25 AM · Half Marathon and 3-Amigos Relay 7:30 AM at Romero's Park, Agua Fría · Santa Fe 5K 8:00 AM at Reunity Resources Farm · Kids 1K Dash 9:00–10:00 AM at Reunity (free, ages 3–12, not chip-timed).
  • Finish, all races: Reunity Resources Farm, 1829 San Ysidro Crossing.
  • Roads into the start close at 7:00 AM. Course support ends at 12:00 PM, the 4 hour 30 minute course limit.
  • Rolling closure: the course closes and reopens in stages as the field passes, so closure times differ from point to point. Per-mile times are in Section 4 and on the course map.
  • Awards at the Reunity finish festival: 5K at 9:45 AM · Half Marathon and Relay at 10:45 AM. The finish festival runs 9:00 AM–1:00 PM.
There is no cutoff on the course. Mile 9 is the turnaround and nothing more. The only limit is the 12:00 PM course close. Never turn a runner back. Runners behind the sweep are offered a ride to the finish; riding is their choice.

2The course

A single-pass loop through Agua Fría village, then out and back along the Santa Fe River Trail, the paved path built by the river restoration.

  1. Start: Romero's Park, Agua Fría (map pin 35.65923, -106.02906). In a maps app, search "Romero Park, Santa Fe"; the street address does not show correctly in navigation apps.
  2. Opening village loop, miles 0–4.9. Run once. Aid stations 1 (mile 2) and 2 (mile 4) are on this loop.
  3. Corridor Entry, mile 4.9. The course joins the Santa Fe River Trail. Relay Exchange Zone 1 is here, at a wide gravel pull-off.
  4. Out along the Santa Fe River Trail. Through San Isidro River Park and Agua Fría Park (mile 6). The trail stretch is a divided two-way runner lane with a centre cone line; runners keep right.
  5. Turnaround, mile 9. Course high point, 6,883 ft. Aid station 5 with ALS medical. Relay Exchange Zone 2.
  6. Return along the same trail, miles 9–13.1. Stations 3 and 4 serve runners again on the way back (miles 10 and 12).
  7. Finish: Reunity Resources Farm (map pin 35.66079, -106.01042). Relay teammates may join for the final 100 meters.

Turn-by-turn route, mile markers and rolling-closure times: santafehalfmarathon.com/course-map/

Station shifts, set by runner pace

Your shift follows the field, not the clock. The rolling closure reaches your station 15 minutes ahead of the first runner, so be set up by then. Your station stays open until the sweep passes. The sweep moves at 20:30 per mile, the pace that reaches the finish just before the 12:00 PM course close.

  • Be ready at the set-up time, not the first-runner time. Wheelchair athletes start at 7:25 AM, and handcyclists can reach you before the first runner does.
  • Stay open until the sweep passes, even when the table goes quiet. The last runners are well behind the pack and need water as much as the leaders did.
  • Alpha and Beta serve both directions at once during the two-way window. Work from the same table and keep runners right of the cone line.
  • For Alpha, the sweep passes outbound at 9:33 AM and comes back at 11:36 AM. For Beta, outbound at 10:14 AM and back at 10:55 AM. Only the return pass closes the station.

Medical coverage

  • Medical tents at the start (Romero's Park) and the finish (Reunity Resources Farm), with ALS medical at the finish.
  • Medic at mile 4 (Station 2) · medical kit at Station Beta (miles 8 and 10) · ALS medical at the mile-9 turnaround (Station 5).
  • A public safety team is positioned about every 2 miles along the course. Emergency response corridors are coordinated with the City of Santa Fe; traffic control holds or escorts vehicles inside closure sectors.

What volunteers should know on race morning

  • Race-morning packet pickup: 6:00–7:00 AM at Romero's Park, for Half Marathon and Relay runners only. 5K runners must collect at the expo.
  • Gear bags: drop 6:00–7:00 AM at Romero's Park; trucked to the finish; collect at Reunity Resources Farm by 1:00 PM.
  • Parking: Half and Relay at Romero's Park · 5K at Reunity Resources Farm · third lot at San Isidro Church, across the street from Reunity. It is about a 20-minute walk (1.1 miles) between the lots.
  • Shuttle: one Santa Fe Trails bus loops the three lots about every 15 minutes, 6:00–7:45 AM (last departure 7:30 AM) and 10:00–11:30 AM (last pickup 11:15 AM from Romero's Park). ADA boarding assistance is provided. Any race barricade or road closure on Agua Fria Street or at a park entrance must let the shuttle through.
  • Relay exchanges are at mile 4.9 (Corridor Entry) and mile 9 (Turnaround). Legs are 4.9, 4.1 and 4.1 miles. The sash is handed over inside the marked chute, and both runners stay in the chute during the handoff.
  • On course: strollers are welcome; no pets.
  • At the finish: finisher medals and age-group medallions are made by Maasai artisans. The post-race green chile brunch is prepared by Santa Fe YouthWorks.

Volunteer thank-yous: exclusive volunteer shirt, breakfast and lunch for race-day volunteers, finish-festival access, and a certificate of participation. Registration deadline to guarantee your shirt size: Wednesday, September 16, 2026.

6Communication and incident protocol

Radio

Incident levels

  1. Minor (cuts, scrapes, questions): handle at the station with the first aid kit, and log it in Part 3.
  2. Course issues (a runner off course, crowding, public trail users in the runner lane): radio race command with your station number or nearest crossing, and alert the nearest course marshal.
  3. Emergency (serious injury, a runner in medical distress, an immediate safety threat): call 911 first, then radio race command with your exact location so crews can clear the path at the nearest crossing.
Nearest medical help: medic at mile 4 (Station 2) · medical kit at Station Beta (miles 8 and 10) · ALS medical at the mile-9 turnaround (Station 5) · medical tents at the start and the finish, with ALS medical at the finish.
  • Never turn a runner back. There is no cutoff on the course. A runner who wants to stop can ride with the sweep, or go to the nearest aid station.
  • Tell runners who feel unwell to stop at the nearest aid station or flag the nearest volunteer.
  • Confirm: law-enforcement, search-and-rescue and bike-patrol staffing, and how volunteers contact them. No race source confirms these yet.

7Volunteer waiver

No volunteer waiver has been written yet. The race director needs to supply one approved by the race's attorney, and decide whether volunteers sign it when they register or at check-in. A parent or guardian signs for volunteers under 18.

The two waivers on file are both written for runners, and neither fits volunteers. One asks the signer to certify that a licensed medical doctor has verified their fitness. The other is a draft marked for attorney review. This guide does not reproduce or write waiver language.
