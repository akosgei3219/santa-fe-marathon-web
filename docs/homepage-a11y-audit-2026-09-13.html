The page changed during the audit. A sixth FAQ, "When does registration close?", appeared between the desktop and mobile passes, so a deploy landed mid-audit. None of the findings below depend on that section.

In order

Fix first

  1. Keep two sitewide link rules off the homepage. They override the page's own link colors and leave contact details and policy links at 1.8 to 2.6 to 1. The colors the page asks for already pass.
  2. Win two cascade fights. One CSS rule each restores readable navigation labels and the designed hero button label. Both are already specified in the site's own stylesheet and lose on specificity.
  3. Give the focus ring a light color on navy. Seventeen of the 68 keyboard stops show a ring below 3 to 1, forced by the same kit stylesheet.
  4. Trap and return focus in the Race Concierge. Keyboard users currently fall out of the dialog, then land at the top of the page.
  5. Add a pause to the sponsor ticker and state to the dropdowns. Both are Level A or AA failures on high-traffic parts of the page.
  6. Fix the 320 px header. The menu button is half off-screen at 400 percent zoom and on the narrowest phones.
Principle 1

Perceivable

Critical1.4.3 Contrast

Sitewide link rules repaint the homepage's contact and policy links nearly invisible

The page sets its own colors on these links: the footer email and phone use the sunset token, the policy links inherit sand, and the hotel phone uses cream. Two !important rules written for WordPress content on light pages override all of them. The Elementor kit stylesheet's html body p a:not(...) rule forces #B9461E, and a rule in the WordPress Customizer's Additional CSS, html body main a:not(...), forces #963312. On navy #26265E the hotel phone measures 1.83 to 1, the Privacy Policy and Refund & Deferral Policy links 1.96 to 1, and the footer email and race phone 2.60 to 1. All of this was re-checked on a fresh load with no running transitions.

FixScope both rules to inner pages by prefixing them with html body:not(.home). The homepage's intended colors then return: footer email and phone at 4.64 to 1, policy links at 8.0 to 1, hotel phone at 13.1 to 1.

Major1.4.3 Contrast

Desktop navigation labels sit on crimson pills at 2.74 to 1

The Elementor kit rule .elementor-kit-5 button {background-color:#CC0033; border-radius:999px} outranks the React button's bg-transparent class. The Races, Runner Info and Experience Santa Fe render as crimson pills with #222222 text. #CC0033 is also the retired pre-rebrand crimson.

FixAdd #root .navgrp > button {background-color:transparent; border-radius:0} to custom.css.

Major1.4.3 Contrast

The hero "Register for 2026" label measures 3.06 to 1

Cream #FFF8EF text sits on orange #FF5400 at 20 px regular, which needs 4.5 to 1. The stylesheet already sets .hero-cta-solid {color:#0C0C0E}, which would measure 6.07 to 1. The rescue rule html body #root a {color:inherit} wins on specificity, so the label inherits cream from the hero.

FixAdd html body #root a.hero-cta-solid {color:#0C0C0E}.

Major1.4.11 Non-text contrast

The focus ring drops below 3 to 1 on every navy band

Every control gets a 2 px #B9461E ring with a soft halo. On light bands it measures 4.55 to 5.31 to 1. On navy it measures 2.60 to 1, and 2.37 to 1 on #2D2D63. That covers 17 of 68 stops: both hero buttons, results search and pagination, the hotel contacts and code buttons, Register Youth Athlete, the sponsor button, the newsletter form and every footer link.

FixThe link ring comes from the kit rule a:focus, a:focus-visible {outline-color:#B9461E !important}. Inside navy sections, override it with outline-color:#FBF9F6 !important, which measures 13.1 to 1, and give buttons the same light ring.

Major1.4.10 Reflow

At 320 px the menu button and two cards are cut off

At 320 px wide, which matches 1280 px at 400 percent zoom, the header controls end at 349 px. The menu button shows 29 of its 58 px. The MAC Framework cards "The Mission" and "The Vision" carry min-w-[320px] and end at 340 px. The page suppresses horizontal scrolling, so the cut-off parts cannot be reached.

FixHide the header Register button below 360 px, since the hero button and the bottom Register bar already cover it. Change min-w-[320px] to min-w-0 on those cards.

Minor1.4.3 Contrast

Orange tier labels and road tags miss by a hair

#E07B39 at 10 to 11 px bold measures 4.01 to 1 on #313166 for the Title, Gold and Silver Sponsor labels, 17 instances. It measures 4.21 to 4.24 to 1 on #352D5B and #2D2D63 for driving-guide tags such as "Strict Vehicle Lockdown", 8 instances.

FixUse #F19A55, which measures 5.4 to 5.7 to 1 on those grounds.

Minor1.3.1 Info and relationships

The page banner is the hero, not the navigation

The hero is the page's <header>, labelled with the same words as the h1. The navigation sits in a separate sticky container outside it, so the banner landmark skips the site's own menu.

FixWrap the sticky bar in <header> and make the hero a <section aria-labelledby> that points at the h1.

Major1.4.13 Hover or focus
4.1.2 Name, role, value

Desktop dropdowns can't be dismissed and don't report their state

The three menus open on hover or focus through CSS alone. With focus on "The Races", a real Escape keypress left the panel open over the hero. Clicking the button does nothing, and it carries no aria-expanded or aria-controls, so screen readers never hear "expanded" or "collapsed". Keyboard users also pass through all 21 dropdown links before reaching the page.

FixDrive each panel from state: click toggles it, aria-expanded mirrors it, Escape closes it and leaves focus on the trigger. The skip link already covers the long tab path.

Principle 2

Operable

Major2.4.3 Focus order

The Race Concierge lets focus escape, then loses it on close

The dialog declares aria-modal="true", but Tab from the last suggestion, "Post-race burritos", moved to "Register for 2026" behind the overlay and kept going through the page. After Escape closed the dialog, focus sat on <body> instead of the magnifier button.

FixIn the Concierge component, wrap Tab and Shift+Tab inside the dialog card. Store the focused element when the dialog opens and return focus to it on close.

Major2.2.2 Pause, stop, hide

The sponsor ticker never stops for keyboard or touch users

The strip scrolls on a 26-second infinite loop and pauses only under a hovering mouse. The reduced-motion setting does stop it, but there is no control on the page. This is a Level A criterion.

FixAdd a Pause button with aria-pressed, and pause on :focus-within as well as :hover.

Minor2.2.2 Pause, stop, hide

The countdown ticks every second with no way to pause

role="timer" correctly keeps screen readers quiet, but the seconds digit changes continuously beside the hero content.

FixShow days, hours and minutes only, which also makes the update infrequent enough to stop drawing the eye.

Minor2.4.3 Focus order

On phones, the notice "Details" button hides itself and drops focus

Activating "Race Day Notice Details" reveals the notice text and removes the button, so keyboard and screen reader focus falls back to the top of the document.

FixIn AlertBanner, move focus to the revealed notice with tabindex="-1", or keep the button as a show and hide toggle.

Principle 3

Understandable

Minor2.5.3 Label in name
3.1.2 Language of parts

The language button's spoken name doesn't contain "ES"

The button shows "ES" but is named "Cambiar a español", and "Switch to English" when it shows "EN". Speech-input users who say "click ES" may get no match. The Spanish phrase has no lang attribute, so screen readers pronounce it with the English voice. The page language itself switches correctly.

FixName it aria-label="ES, cambiar a español" with lang="es", and use the matching English pattern in the EN state.

Principle 4

Robust

Minor4.1.2 Name, role, value

Race tabs ignore arrow keys and point at panels that don't exist

The four race buttons use role="tab", which tells screen reader users to use arrow keys, but ArrowRight did nothing. Each is a separate Tab stop. The three unselected tabs point aria-controls at panels React has not rendered.

FixAdd a roving tabindex with arrow-key selection, or drop the tab roles and use buttons with aria-pressed.

Minor4.1.2 Name, role, value

The menu button points at a menu that exists only when open

aria-controls="mobile-menu" refers to an element React renders only after the button is pressed, so the reference is broken while the menu is closed.

FixAlways render the menu container and toggle its hidden attribute.

Minor4.1.2 Name, role, value

The mobile aid-station table is a tab stop with no name

On phones the table scrolls sideways, and the browser makes its scroll container a keyboard stop. It has no role or label, so screen readers can't say what it is.

FixGive the scroll container role="region", aria-label="Aid stations" and tabindex="0".

Verified

What already works

  • One h1, no skipped heading levels, and the event name is in the h1 for screen readers.
  • All 27 images carry alt text; sponsor logos are correctly empty where the name is printed beside them.
  • The skip link is the first stop, appears on focus, and lands on main.
  • Every control shows a visible focus ring, and tab order follows the visual order.
  • Results search, newsletter email and the Concierge input all have labels.
  • The FAQ accordion uses buttons with aria-expanded and aria-controls.
  • The Concierge is a labelled dialog that moves focus in and closes on Escape.
  • Switching to Spanish sets the page language to es.
  • The countdown uses role="timer", and pagination announces its page politely.
  • Text-spacing overrides clip nothing, and nothing scrolls sideways at 375 px.
  • Pinch zoom is allowed, and the reduced-motion setting stops all animation.
  • The open mobile menu keeps all 21 links reachable even though it is taller than the screen.

Advisories, not counted as failures

  • Hero text sits on photographs. The mobile crop puts the date line over a runner's white shirt. Contrast over photos needs a manual check.
  • No scroll padding for the sticky bars. The 72 px header and the 69 px mobile Register bar can cover focused elements and anchor targets, which WCAG 2.2 treats as a failure.
  • The mobile menu doesn't close on Escape. Not required for an inline disclosure menu, but expected.
  • Seven links open new tabs without saying so. The Register buttons send runners to RunSignup in a new tab.
  • Six targets are under 44 px. The race tabs are 39 px tall and the hotel phone links 26 px. All pass WCAG 2.2's 24 px minimum; 44 px is an AAA goal in 2.1.

If the redesign goes ahead

  • Prefer a still photo. A background video loop needs a visible pause control under 2.2.2.
  • Measure the overlay, don't assume it. A 0.4 to 0.6 black overlay does not guarantee 4.5 to 1 for white text over the brightest part of a photo.
  • Keep the hero button label dark on orange. Near-black on #FF5400 measures 6.07 to 1; cream on #AD4A2E terracotta measures 5.26 to 1. Cream on orange is today's 3.06 failure.
  • Name the four Register buttons per race. "Register for the Santa Fe 5K" keeps screen reader link lists from showing four identical entries.
  • Put the stats in real text. Numbers stay readable and translatable; decorative icons get aria-hidden.
  • Name each organization in the charity grid's alt text. A logo alone tells a screen reader nothing.
Scope

Method and limits

  • Live homepage, logged out, in Chromium at 1280 by 800, 375 by 812 and 320 by 640.
  • Contrast was computed for 390 visible text elements, and focus-ring contrast for every desktop tab stop. The failing links, nav buttons and hero button were re-checked on a fresh load with no running CSS transitions, because frozen transitions have produced false contrast readings on this site before.
  • Tab, Shift+Tab, Escape and arrow keys were sent as real keypresses. The test browser could not send Enter or Space, so activation was confirmed with clicks.
  • Not measured: text contrast over the hero photographs, and whether sticky bars cover focused elements, because the test browser did not scroll on Tab. Both need a manual pass.
  • Real assistive technology was not used. A VoiceOver pass on an iPhone is the highest-value follow-up before race day.
